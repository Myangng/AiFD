Artist statement

When I started this patch, I wanted the community to be the main focus instead of making the telescope the biggest object. That is why I placed a planet in the middle with a small house and people inside it. To me, that little scene represents Baruch as a place where students meet, learn from each other, and find their own direction.

I put the telescope near the edge, pointing toward the planet, to make it feel like someone is discovering our community from far away. The gold rings move around the center and bring the different parts of the design together. I like that they make the patch feel active even though it is a still image. The tiny stars leave some open space so the design does not feel too crowded.

I kept the background dark navy so the blue planet and gold lines would stand out. The round shape, curved lettering, and double border make it feel like a patch that could actually go on a mission jacket. I named it “Discovery at Baruch” because the design is about noticing the people and experiences around us, not just exploring outer space.

AI disclosure

I used AI to help code and troubleshoot my HTML mission patch generator. It helped turn my ideas into different patch layouts and working controls for the colors, lettering, stars, and other details. I tested the variations, adjusted the settings, and picked Design 03, “Orbiting Together,” as my final version. The concept of a telescope discovering the Baruch community and the choice of the final composition came from my own decisions.
