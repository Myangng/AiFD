# Brand Skills — Rhode NYC / Your City. Your Shade.

## Status and campaign idea
Unofficial fictional student campaign, not an official Rhode collaboration. Spring 2027 concept: four limited-edition lip tints inspired by New York City neighborhoods. Campaign line: **Your City. Your Shade.** Celebrate effortless beauty, self-expression, and the individuality of NYC.

## Core identity
- Brand/campaign: Rhode NYC — The NYC Lip Collection.
- Neighborhood shades: **SoHo Rose** (romantic muted rose, #A34857), **Brooklyn Mocha** (warm brownstone mocha, #824B40), **Manhattan Blush** (subtle polished rosy nude), **Queens Glow** (luminous soft neutral pink).
- Colors from the approved style guide: Warm Cream **#F4E9E4**, Soft Blush **#D9A5A0**, SoHo Rose **#A34857**, Mocha **#824B40**, Dark Brown **#292321**.
- Typeface: **DM Sans**, bold 700 for confident, minimal headlines; regular 400 for body copy. Restrained lowercase wordmark treatment; generous negative space.
- Mood: minimal, modern, quiet luxury, editorial, youthful, intimate, understated, authentic NYC, effortlessly cool, glossy but not flashy.

## Photography direction
- Photorealistic luxury beauty editorial, tactile and believable, natural skin texture, subtle lip gloss sheen, refined product surfaces, rich but controlled detail.
- Lighting: warm diffused daylight, soft overcast window light, gentle directional glow; soft shadows, no harsh flash.
- Angles: tight macro lip/product detail, eye-level portraits, three-quarter product hero, overhead flat lay, cinematic street-level lifestyle and architectural details. Composition should leave purposeful negative space for copy.
- Settings: NYC sidewalks, brownstone stoops, café windows, subway tile and entrances, SoHo cast-iron façades, city corners and editorial studio backdrops. Use locations as subtle atmosphere, not tourist clichés.
- Materials: translucent gloss, polished packaging, glass reflections, cream paper, muted stone, warm concrete, brushed metal.
- Models: diverse adult NYC residents with realistic skin, candid expressions and unforced styling. Wardrobe neutral, effortless and contemporary.

## Brand consistency rules
- Every generated image should clearly fit the warm cream, blush, rose, mocha, and dark-brown palette. No loud neon, saturated blue, acid green or harsh high-contrast filters.
- Beauty remains the focus; NYC is a visual context, not an overwhelming backdrop.
- Product packaging should look realistic, correctly proportioned, and physically plausible. For accurate brand logos/labels, composite real approved artwork afterward instead of trusting generated text.
- For print layouts, keep clear space for a later headline and campaign mark; ask the generator not to render words or logos into the image.
- Avoid cartoon/illustration aesthetics, plastic skin, distorted faces/hands, warped gloss tubes, messy collages, heavy glamour, excessive props, generic stock-photo smiles, obvious tourist imagery, and clutter.

## Prompt builder behavior
Every prompt must combine: image type + subject + selected neighborhood shade + selected camera angle + chosen NYC setting + lighting + brand palette + editorial mood + composition + target aspect ratio/dimensions + negative constraints. Provide different image types: product shot, lifestyle portrait, lip/detail macro, NYC setting, editorial collage. Supported ad formats: Instagram portrait 4:5 (1080×1350), square 1:1 (1080×1080), Reel/Story 9:16 (1080×1920), billboard concept 16:9 (1920×1080), magazine portrait 3:4 (2400×3200), NYC subway poster concept 2:3 (2000×3000). Actual print production sizes and bleed depend on vendor specifications.

## Image review checklist
1. Palette matches five style-guide colors.
2. Lighting is soft and natural; skin and products look believable.
3. The image feels both NYC-specific and minimalist.
4. The chosen shade/subject and camera angle are obvious.
5. There is sufficient empty space for ad typography.
6. No fake or misspelled wordmark is baked into the image.
7. The format is appropriate for its intended placement.

## Iteration protocol
Generate at least one product shot, one lifestyle image, and one detail or setting image. Compare each to the checklist. If images drift, update this document's directions and corresponding prompt templates, then regenerate.
