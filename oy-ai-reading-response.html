Oy, A.I. Reading Response

After reading “Oy, A.I.” by Jaron Lanier, I thought it was interesting how he explained that AI comes from human knowledge and creativity. Before reading this article, I never really thought about how much human work is behind AI. I also think it is becoming harder to tell what is real and what is generated by AI, especially on social media. Even though AI makes things easier for us, I think it is important to not rely on it too much.

I can relate this article to what I have been doing in this class. For my Rhode campaign, I used AI to help me generate images, create websites, and explore different design ideas. However, there were many times when AI did not give me the results I wanted, so I had to keep making changes until I was satisfied with the design. This made me realize that even though AI is helpful, we still need our own creativity and ideas. I think AI is a useful tool for designers, but it should not completely replace the creative process.

Reading: “Oy, A.I.” by Jaron Lanier
